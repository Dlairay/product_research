🔍 Prompt:
What is the aftercare guide for this chair?

🧠 Answer:
The aftercare guide for the Secretlab chair includes the following instructions:

1. Minimize exposure to perspiration: Perspiration is acidic and can degrade the leatherette’s top coating. Regularly wipe down your chair with a slightly damp cloth to remove perspiration and dirt.

2. Keep away from fluids: Moisture is detrimental to the chair’s upholstery. Clean up any spills immediately and only sit on the chair after you have completely dried off from a shower.

3. Avoid abuse: Keep the chair away from impacts, drops, and sharp objects to prevent damage.

4. Keep away from direct heat: Exposure to high heat can compromise the leatherette’s top coating, affecting its durability, shine, or color. Store the chair in a cool and dry place.

5. Keep pets away: Claws and teeth can damage the upholstery and other soft parts of the chair.

6. Avoid direct contact with bare skin: Body oil and friction from direct skin contact can degrade the protective coating over time. Avoid constant contact with bare skin, especially in warm, humid environments.

7. Use appropriate cleaning products: While a damp cloth is usually sufficient, specially formulated upholstery cleaners and wipes are recommended for thorough cleaning.
